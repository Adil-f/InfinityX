Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What technologies do you use?

We use the latest technologies including React, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, and cloud platforms like AWS and Azure. Our stack is always evolving to stay current with industry best practices.

How long does a project typically take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. A simple website might take 2-4 weeks, while complex applications can take 3-6 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, we offer various support packages including maintenance, updates, and 24/7 monitoring. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients.

Can you work with existing systems?

Absolutely! We have extensive experience integrating with existing systems and APIs. We can work with your current infrastructure or help you migrate to new platforms.

What is your development process?

We follow an agile methodology with regular client communication. Our process includes discovery, design, development, testing, and deployment phases with continuous feedback loops.
